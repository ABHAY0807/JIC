Why should I buy insurance, and how does it protect my family?+

Insurance acts as your primary financial safety net against life’s unpredictable emergencies—such as medical hospitalizations, sudden accidents, premature loss of an earning family member, or damage to your vehicle and business assets.

Instead of depleting your lifelong savings, emergency funds, or taking high-interest loans during a crisis, an appropriate insurance policy transfers that financial risk to the insurer—guaranteeing that your family’s health, lifestyle, and financial future remain secure.

Can I purchase insurance online myself, and why choose JIC?+

While buying online via apps is possible, automated portals rarely highlight critical fine print—such as room-rent sub-limits, disease-specific waiting periods, proportional deductions, co-pay clauses, or vehicle depreciation rules that lead to unexpected claim rejections.

At Jagruti Insurance Consultancy (JIC), led by G.H. Solanki with 30+ years of advisory experience, we evaluate your health profile and budget to select the right cover with zero bias. Crucially, when an emergency strikes, you have an experienced personal advisor managing your claim documentation, hospital approvals, and insurer follow-ups.

How are insurance premiums calculated, and how can I keep costs affordable?+

Premiums are determined by key underwriting factors: entry age, sum insured, pre-existing medical conditions, city of residence, policy term, and selected add-on riders. For vehicles, it is based on vehicle age, cubic capacity (CC), and Insured Declared Value (IDV).

Ways to optimize your premium:

  • Buy Early: Starting health and term life policies young locks in lower lifetime premiums.
  • Use Super Top-ups: Enhance your health cover (e.g. ₹5 Lakh base + ₹20 Lakh Super Top-up) at a fraction of the cost of a high base policy.
  • Maintain No-Claim Bonus (NCB): Earn up to a 50% discount on vehicle own-damage premiums and bonus coverage on health policies for claim-free years.
  • Multi-Year Discounts: Opting for 2 or 3-year policy terms often provides upfront premium discounts of 7.5% to 15%.

What is the difference between Cashless and Reimbursement claims?+

1. Cashless Hospitalization: Available at the insurer’s network hospitals. The hospital’s TPA desk directly submits your pre-authorization request. Once approved, the insurance company settles eligible medical bills directly with the hospital, and you only pay for non-medical items.

2. Reimbursement Claim: If admitted to a non-network hospital or in an emergency where cashless was not feasible, you pay the bills at discharge. You then submit original bills, discharge summary, reports, and claim forms to the insurer for reimbursement within 15–30 days. We guide you step-by-step through both processes.

When should I renew my insurance, and what happens if my policy lapses?+

You should always renew your policy 7 to 15 days before the exact expiry date to ensure continuous protection:

  • Health Insurance: A 30-day grace period is provided to pay the premium without losing waiting-period continuity benefits; however, any illness or hospitalization occurring during the lapsed days is strictly NOT covered.
  • Motor Insurance: Driving with an expired policy is illegal under the Motor Vehicles Act. A break in policy requires vehicle reinspection and risks the permanent loss of your accumulated No-Claim Bonus (NCB) after 90 days.

JIC sends timely renewal alerts well in advance so your policies never lapse.

What should I do if an insurance company raises queries or rejects my claim?+

If an insurer delays, deducts, or rejects a claim, do not panic. Many initial rejections arise from missing hospital documentation, billing code discrepancies, or misunderstood medical histories.

At JIC, we examine the exact rejection letter and policy clauses, obtain necessary clarifying certificates from your treating doctor, and file a formal representation to the insurer’s Grievance Redressal Committee. If needed, we guide you through escalating the dispute to the Insurance Ombudsman for an independent, fair resolution.

What documents are required to buy or renew an insurance policy?+

Documentation is simple, streamlined, and digital:

  • Health & Life: Photo ID & Address Proof (Aadhaar, PAN Card, or Passport), passport-sized photograph, proposer bank details / cancelled cheque (for direct claim settlements), and recent medical reports (if required by age/cover sum).
  • Motor Insurance: Previous policy copy, Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), and transfer papers (if pre-owned vehicle).
  • Business Insurance: GST Registration certificate, commercial premise address proof, and asset/stock inventory statements.
